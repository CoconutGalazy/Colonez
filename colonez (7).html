How to play

You are the colony, not a character. There is no avatar to walk around — you command settlers.

  • Move the view — W A S D, or drag with the middle/right mouse button. Scroll to zoom. Double-click to recentre.
  • Select settlers — left-click one, or left-drag a box around several. Shift-click to add to the selection.
  • Give orders — right-click: on ground to march there, on an enemy to attack it, on a tree or rock to go gather it.
  • Change layer — click Sky / Surface / Depths on the left to look there. With settlers selected, they will travel there themselves: riding a water current up, or digging their way down.
  • Everything goes to one store — settlers carry what they gather back to your Storehouse (or Town Hall) automatically.
  • Build — the Build button, then click a spot. Locked buttons need a building first.

Build

Buildings take the architecture of the biome they stand in: adobe in the desert, igloos and spruce lodges in the arctic, stilted boardwalks on water and swamp, thatch and timber elsewhere. Structures must sit inside your territory. Locked recipes open with season tiers.

Season pass

Kingdom EXP comes from quests, achievements, harvests and kills. Each tier unlocks recipes and raises your passive defense multiplier. Guards reach commander rank at tier 5.

Controls

WASD move, hold Shift to run (uses stamina). Walk into water to swim - stamina drains, and if it hits zero you drown. Mouse wheel zooms.
Your weapon points at the cursor. Left click uses it: hit an enemy in reach, or take any tree, rock, ore or plant within 6 tiles. The wave on the hotbar slot is the reload. 1-5 pick a slot (slot 1 is fists; draw a weapon to do real damage). Click a settler to assign work.
Hold Z on land to dig down to the Depths (it leaves a shaft). Swim onto a rising water current and hold Q to reach the Sky. Hold Q at a shaft to climb back up; hold Z on a sky isle to drop to the surface.
B build, E items, M map (click a spot, A to travel), F allies, P season pass, O settings, Tab leaderboard, Enter chat (/help for commands), Esc cancel. Bosses sleep at black obelisks and drop unique weapons.
