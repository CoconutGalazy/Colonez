Build

Buildings take the architecture of the biome they stand in: adobe in the desert, igloos and spruce lodges in the arctic, stilted boardwalks on water and swamp, thatch and timber elsewhere. Structures must sit inside your territory. Locked recipes open with season tiers.

Controls

WASD move. Mouse wheel zooms. Click ground to walk, click a tree, rock or ore to gather, click a beast or raider to fight, click a settler to assign work, click a ship to sail.
1-5 hotbar (slot 1 is fists; draw a weapon to do real damage). B build, E materials, M map, F allies, P season pass, Q ascend / Z descend a layer, Esc cancel.
