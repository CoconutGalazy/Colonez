Build

Buildings take the architecture of the biome they stand in: adobe in the desert, igloos and spruce lodges in the arctic, stilted boardwalks on water and swamp, thatch and timber elsewhere. Structures must sit inside your territory. Locked recipes open with season tiers.

Controls

Mouse wheel zooms. Click a settler to assign work.
WASD is the only way to move - there is no click-to-move. Left click uses what you are holding: hit an enemy in reach, chop a tree or rock beside you, board a ship, talk to a neighbour's hall. 1-5 pick a hotbar slot (slot 1 is fists; draw a weapon to do real damage).
B build, E items, M map (click a spot, A to travel), F allies, P season pass, Q ascend / Z descend a layer, Enter chat, Esc cancel. Bosses sleep at black obelisks - one per region - and drop unique weapons.
